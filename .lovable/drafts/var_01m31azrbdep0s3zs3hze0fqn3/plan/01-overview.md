# Commercial contracts website expansion

## Goal
Reposition Six Harmony around recurring commercial cleaning, facilities management, and property maintenance across all six service locations, with a clear path to request a free site assessment or submit a tender enquiry.

## What will change
- Rework the home page so Commercial Cleaning and Facilities Management lead the offer.
- Replace the current animated figures with practical capability signals that do not rely on unverified numbers.
- Add Commercial Cleaning to the main menu and make the menu work across separate pages.
- Add dedicated pages for:
  - Commercial Cleaning
  - Facilities Management
  - Commercial Property Maintenance
- Cover offices, retail, property management, body corporates, industrial and warehouses, education, healthcare, hospitality, and construction.
- Detail daily cleaning, deep cleaning, construction/final cleans, windows, carpets, floors, one-off work, and scheduled programmes.
- Detail planned and reactive maintenance, cleaning, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, painting, carpentry, inspections, and contractor management.
- Add prominent “Request a Free Site Assessment” actions throughout the site.
- Add case study, testimonial, and tender/procurement sections without inventing client outcomes or endorsements.
- Add a structured enquiry form that prepares an email to `admin@sixharmony.co.nz`, capturing company and site details, services, frequency, and preferred start date.
- Give every page unique search and sharing information, including nationwide service coverage and Auckland relevance within page content, plus self-referencing canonical URLs for `sixharmony.co.nz`.

## Content integrity
NZBN, insurance, health and safety systems, staff training/vetting, environmental practices, named clients, and testimonials were not supplied. The site will not claim these as facts. Credibility areas will use neutral procurement-ready wording and clear prompts for verified documentation, ready to be replaced when details are supplied.

## Validation
Check all new navigation, forms, email handoff, mobile layout, page metadata, and reduced-motion behaviour in the live preview.
